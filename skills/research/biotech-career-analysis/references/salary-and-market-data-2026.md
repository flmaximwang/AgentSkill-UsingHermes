## Key Market Facts (2026)
- AIDD cross-disciplinary talent < 500 people nationwide
- ADC pipeline share at IND: > 30% (was 18% three years ago)
- CGT pipeline share: ~15%
- Mid-level talent gap: 2022-2024 layoffs created a "lost cohort" → 3-8yr experienced scientists are premium
- CXO → Biotech跳槽: typical 25-30% raise
- Average hiring cycle for ADC process director: 4-6 months
