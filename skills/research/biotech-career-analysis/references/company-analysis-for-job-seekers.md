# Company Analysis for Job Seekers

> Full methodology for analyzing biotech/pharma companies from a job-seeker's perspective (not investor).

## Core classification: What does this company *actually* do?

| Type | Revenue model | Examples |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **CRO** (Contract Research Organization) | Service fees for experiments | 药明康德, 康龙化成 |
| **CDMO** (Contract Development & Manufacturing) | Production fees | 药明生物, 金斯瑞蓬勃生物 |
| **AI-CRO** (AI-powered research services) | Service fees using AI tools | 晶泰科技, 深势科技 |
| **AI-Biotech** (in-house drug pipelines) | Drug sales or licensing (if successful) | 英矽智能, 百图生科 |
| **Tools & Reagents** | Selling kits, instruments, synthesis | 金斯瑞基因合成, 义翘神州 |

**Job-seeker significance**: CRO/CDMO = many employees, standardized roles, clear promotion ladder, lower salary. AI-Biotech = higher salary, more risk, fewer openings.

## Three-step workflow

### Step 1 — Website "Business" page (3 min)
Official website → Business/Solutions page. Note:
- Listed business segments
- Technology platform (one sentence)
- Revenue source

### Step 2 — Job postings on BOSS直聘/猎聘 (5 min)
- Count roles matching the user's skillset (protein engineering, computational biology, CADD)
- Note tech stack in JDs
- Check posting recency

### Step 3 — One analytical article (10 min)
Search "XXX 深度分析" or "XXX 招股书解读". Read:
- Actual business model vs website claims (often differ)
- Customer base (big pharma vs biotech startups)
- Competitors
- Financial health (profitable vs burning cash)

### Decision
Combine all three: worth applying? Which angle to emphasize?

## Example: 晶泰科技 (full walkthrough)

1. **Website**: "AI + quantum physics + robotic automation drug discovery platform"
2. **Job postings**: Computational chemist roles, small-molecule CADD, Python — not protein engineering
3. **Analytical article (虎嗅)**: Revenue is 50% drug discovery service + 50% solid-state research (crystal prediction). Loss-making. Competes with 药明康德 for CRO customers. Small-molecule focused.
4. **Decision for protein engineer**: Low direct match — protein design experience is not primary need. Only apply if JD explicitly mentions protein engineering.

## Warning: Website != Reality

| Website claim | Often actually is |
|--------------|-----------------|
| "AI制药平台" | AI-CRO service provider |
| "蛋白质设计平台" | CDMO/protein production service |
| "端到端新药发现" | Early-stage screening only, no full pipeline |
| "计算+实验一体化" | Mostly experimental, small compute team |

Check job postings — they reveal what the company *actually spends money on*.
